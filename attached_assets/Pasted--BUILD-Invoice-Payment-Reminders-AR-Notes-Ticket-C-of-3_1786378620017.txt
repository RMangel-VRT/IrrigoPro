# BUILD — Invoice Payment Reminders + AR Notes

**Ticket C of 3.** Preceded by `BUILD - Bookkeeper Role + Central Role Registry (A of 3)` and `BUILD - AR Collections View on the Invoice List (B of 3)`.

**What & Why**
A bookkeeper can now see who owes money (ticket B) but has no way to act on it inside IrrigoPro, and nowhere to record that she did. Reminders happen in a personal inbox and the follow-up history lives in someone's head.

This ticket adds a payment reminder that IrrigoPro sends, a record of every reminder that went out, and an internal AR note thread per invoice.

**This is the only ticket of the three that can reach a customer's inbox.** Everything below is built around that: refusal rules, a throttle, and a confirmation list that is not optional.

## Prerequisites

**Tickets A and B.** A provides `CAN_SEND_INVOICE_EMAIL`; B provides the columns and filters the reminder state renders into.

**`BUILD - Decouple Invoice Sent-ness from Lifecycle Status`** — hard prerequisite. The refusal rule below turns on `sentAt`, and today payment overwrites it.

## Current state (confirmed in code)

**Invoice email delivery already exists.** `EmailService.sendInvoiceDetailPdf` (`email-service.ts:1072`) already emails an invoice with its PDF to a customer, behind `POST /api/invoices/:invoiceId/pdf/send` (`routes.ts:12314`). **Reuse it. Do not build a second invoice sender.**

**A throttle and dedup pattern already exists.** `missing_photos_notifications` (`schema.ts:381`) is one row per recipient carrying `lastSentAt` / `lastSentEmailAt` / `lastEmailSheetCount` / `sentByUserId`, used to make a manager-triggered notify action idempotent and to surface "last notified" in the UI. Follow that shape.

**A delivery-address precedent exists.** Estimates record `sentToEmail` (`schema.ts:493`) — the address actually used, stored rather than resolved at read time. Reminders do the same, for the same reason: the customer's email may change afterwards.

**The customer's billing address is on the invoice.** `invoices.customerEmail` (`schema.ts:838`, not null).

## Slice 1 — Schema

**`invoice_reminders`** — one row per reminder **actually sent**:

- `companyId`, `invoiceId`, `sentByUserId`
- `sentAt`, `sentToEmail` — the address used, recorded not resolved
- `reminderNumber` — 1, 2, 3… per invoice
- `templateKey`
- `balanceAtSend`, `daysOverdueAtSend` — a reminder is a historical record and must stay readable after the invoice changes
- `deliveryStatus`, `deliveryError`
- index on `(companyId, invoiceId)`

**`invoice_ar_notes`** — internal follow-up log:

- `companyId`, `invoiceId`, `userId`, `note`, `createdAt`
- **Append-only.** No edit, no delete.
- index on `(companyId, invoiceId)`

**These notes are internal.** They are distinct from `invoices.notes` (which prints on the invoice) and from `qbNote` (QuickBooks cleanup). **They must never appear on a customer-facing document or email.** State that in the code comment where the table is defined, and assert it in a test against generated output.

## Slice 2 — The send path

`POST /api/invoices/:id/reminders`, gated `CAN_SEND_INVOICE_EMAIL`.

**Refuses, with a distinct message for each:**

- Paid, or zero balance
- Terminal status — `cancelled` / `superseded` / `merged`
- Draft
- No `customerEmail`
- `qbVoidDetectedAt` set — QuickBooks shows this voided; resolve that first
- **`sentAt == null`** — see below

**Refusing to remind an invoice that was never sent** (decided) is deliberate, not an edge case. Chasing someone for an invoice they never received is worse than useless. The row's call to action is *send it*, not *remind*. Surface that in the UI as the alternative action.

**Throttle: one reminder per invoice per 7 days**, configurable per company, **enforced server-side**. A throttled request returns **when the next one is allowed**, and the button says so. It must not silently do nothing — that is the failure mode already seen with the payment-sync refresh button, where a throttled click produced no toast, no change, and no explanation, and the button looked broken.

**Recording:**

- Writes an `invoice_reminders` row on success only.
- A failed send records `deliveryStatus: 'failed'` and **does not increment `reminderNumber`** — a bounced email is not a reminder the customer received.

## Slice 3 — Templates

Three tones, keyed to aging, **chosen by the bookkeeper at send time and never automatically**:

| Template | Suggested for |
|---|---|
| Friendly | 1–30 days overdue |
| Firm | 31–60 days |
| Final notice | 60+ days |

The aging bucket suggests a default; the person picks. Each includes invoice number, effective due date, days overdue, balance due, and the attached PDF. Company name, logo, and reply-to come from the company profile, matching the existing estimate email.

Use the same effective-due-date helper extracted in ticket B. Do not re-derive days overdue in the template.

## Slice 4 — Batch send

Select rows on the AR list, then:

1. **A confirmation list is displayed** — every invoice that will be emailed, the address it goes to, and the template. Below it, every selected invoice that will be **skipped, with its reason**.
2. Nothing sends until that list is confirmed.
3. Results report per invoice: sent, skipped (reason), or failed (error).

**The confirmation list is not optional and must not be collapsible-by-default.** This is the one place in the app where a misclick reaches customers.

**No automatic or scheduled reminders in this ticket** (decided). Every reminder is a person deciding to send it. Scheduled dunning is a separate ticket and should not be the first version of this.

## Slice 5 — Surfacing

- The **Last reminder** and **Reminder count** columns from ticket B become live.
- The **Reminder** filter from ticket B becomes live — never reminded / reminded in last N days / reminded 3+ times.
- New flag: **Reminded, still unpaid** — reminders sent and still overdue. This is the escalation queue.
- AR notes appear on the invoice detail and as a hover indicator on the list row.
- Reminder history is visible on the invoice detail: when, by whom, to what address, which template, and the balance at the time.

## Multi-tenancy (Rule 1)

`invoice_reminders` and `invoice_ar_notes` both carry `companyId` and every query filters on it. **The send path resolves the invoice through the company-scoped fetch before doing anything else** — a bookkeeper must not be able to email another company's customer by passing an invoice id. `super_admin` passes an explicit company. Assert this on the response body and on whether the email was attempted, not on rendered output.

## Decisions

- **IrrigoPro sends the reminder emails** (decided), reusing `sendInvoiceDetailPdf`.
- **Reminders are manual; no automatic dunning** (decided).
- **Never remind an invoice that was never sent** (decided) — send it instead.
- **One reminder per invoice per 7 days, server-enforced, and the refusal explains itself** (decided).
- **A failed send does not count as a reminder** (decided).
- **The delivery address is recorded, not resolved at read time** (decided) — same as estimates.
- **Batch send always shows a confirmation list with skip reasons** (decided).
- **AR notes are append-only and internal** (decided) — never customer-facing.

## Done looks like

- A bookkeeper sends a reminder from the AR list, sees it recorded, and cannot send another for seven days.
- She can see every reminder ever sent on an invoice: when, by whom, to what address, and the balance at the time.
- An invoice that was never sent offers *send it*, not *remind*.
- A batch send shows exactly who will be emailed and who will be skipped, and why, before anything goes out.
- AR notes record the follow-up conversation and never leak onto a customer document.
- Nothing sends on a schedule.

## Tests

- Reminder refused for each case, each returning its own message and each a separate test: paid · zero balance · cancelled · superseded · merged · draft · no `customerEmail` · `sentAt == null` · `qbVoidDetectedAt` set.
- Throttle: a second reminder inside 7 days is refused **and reports when the next is allowed**; the first after the window succeeds and increments `reminderNumber`.
- A failed send records `deliveryStatus: 'failed'` and does **not** increment `reminderNumber`; a subsequent successful send is `reminderNumber` 1.
- `balanceAtSend` and `daysOverdueAtSend` are preserved after the invoice balance later changes.
- `sentToEmail` is preserved after the customer's email is later changed.
- Batch send: a mixed selection produces the confirmation list with correct skip reasons, and **no email is attempted before confirmation** — asserted on the mailer, not the UI.
- The **Reminded, still unpaid** flag renders only when reminders exist and the invoice is still overdue.
- AR notes never appear in the invoice PDF or in any customer-facing email — asserted on the generated output.
- AR notes cannot be edited or deleted through any endpoint.
- Days overdue in the email body matches the AR list for the same invoice.
- Company isolation: a bookkeeper in company A cannot send a reminder for a company B invoice, and no email is attempted.

## Out of scope

- Automatic or scheduled dunning.
- Recording payments in IrrigoPro — QBO remains the only writer of payment state.
- SMS reminders. The Twilio path exists for technician outreach (`missing_photos_notifications` :393) and is deliberately not reused here.
- Customer-facing payment portal or payment links.
- Statements or multi-invoice customer summaries — worth doing, separate ticket.
