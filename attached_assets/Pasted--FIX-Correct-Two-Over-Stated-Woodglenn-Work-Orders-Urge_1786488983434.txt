# FIX — Correct Two Over-Stated Woodglenn Work Orders

**Urgent — two completed work orders will bill roughly $8,600 too high if converted before this lands.**

**What & Why**
Two work orders for Woodglenn Squares HOA carry labor hours that are roughly double the correct figure, caused by the pre-#1869 completion prefill. Both are **completed and unbilled** — no billing sheet, no invoice, nothing sent to the customer. This is a correction before the fact, not a credit after it.

The diagnosis is already done and verified against production. This ticket applies a known correction to exactly two rows.

## The cause, confirmed against production data

Estimate 50017 is internally correct: header `total_labor_hours` 72.50, and its line items sum to 72.50 exactly. But `Σ(labor_hours × quantity)` across those same lines is **140.25** — and 140.25 is the number the old completion prefill displayed. The tech accepted it, and 140.00 landed on the work order header.

That is the #1869 bug reproduced exactly: on inspection-derived estimates, `inspection-estimate-items.ts` merges findings by part so `labor_hours` is already a **line total**, and multiplying by quantity double-counts.

## Why 50.00 and not 72.50 for WO-...314

The approved estimate carries 162 line items totalling 72.50 hours. The completed work order carries 144 items totalling 50.00 hours. The 18 missing items sum to **exactly 22.50 hours**, which closes the gap precisely.

Those items are scattered across seven controllers (A, C, D, E, F, I, J) with no clustering, while controllers B, G, and H came through complete. Twelve of the seventeen are leak repairs, with three valves, two wiring issues, and one pressure issue.

That distribution is deferred work, not data loss — a bug dropping rows would take a random cross-section rather than skewing toward leaks and wiring. Completion uses replace-not-append semantics (`c404ba05`), so the work order correctly holds only what the tech submitted as performed. **The work order's own line items are the record of what was done, and they sum to 50.00.**

WO-...62 needs no such judgment: estimate 50020's header is 10.75 and its line sum is 10.75, agreeing exactly, against a header of 22.00.

**This reading was confirmed with Randy before the correction was written.**

## Steps

1. **Register a migration** in the Super Admin registry, following the `repair-qb-void-mispaid` preview → acknowledge → run pattern.
2. **Preview** must show, for both work orders: current and proposed `total_hours`, `labor_subtotal`, and `total_amount`, plus the parts component that stays unchanged, plus a confirmation that each has no `billing_sheet` and no `invoice_id`. **The preview writes nothing.**
3. **Refuse to run if either work order has since been billed.** Re-check for a billing sheet and an invoice at run time, not just at preview. If either has been converted between now and the run, stop and report — the correction path for a billed ticket is different and is not in this ticket.
4. **Apply**: set `total_hours` to the corrected value, recompute `labor_subtotal` as `total_hours × labor_rate`, and recompute `total_amount` as the new labor subtotal plus the existing parts component. Do not alter the parts figures, the line items, the status, or `completed_at`.
5. **Idempotent.** Running twice changes nothing after the first. Key on the current value so an already-corrected row is skipped rather than re-corrected.
6. **Verify** with the gate query below and attach the output.

## Multi-tenancy (Rule 1)

## Decisions

- **Correct to the work order's own line-item sum** (decided, confirmed with Randy) — the line items are the record of work performed.
- **Do not restore the 18 missing items** (decided) — that work was not done. It is the subject of `BUILD - Follow-Up Work Order for Incomplete Items`, not this ticket.
- **Refuse if billed** (decided) — a billed ticket needs the invoice correction flow, which is out of scope here.
- **Parts are untouched** (decided) — only labor was overstated.
