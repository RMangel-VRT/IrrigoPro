## Merge notes

`manager-workspace.tsx` and `field-tech-dashboard.tsx` are **byte-identical** to their pre-rollback state and re-apply cleanly.

`App.tsx` (+50/-1) and `nav-config.ts` (+87/-3) have drifted — the bookkeeper role and the location-report group landed since. Re-apply the route and nav entries by hand against current structure; do not restore whole files.

At `608393a0` the nav leaf appeared in three places, and each must be re-placed in its current equivalent:

- the billing group, between Manager Workspace and Financial Pulse
- `managerNav`, as the second item directly under Manager Workspace
- the admin group, between Manager Workspace and Financial Pulse

The route was registered in three role blocks in `App.tsx`. Current `App.tsx` has more blocks than it did in August. **Register `/budget-status` in every block whose role can reach the nav entry** — a nav item pointing at an unregistered route is a Rule 3 orphan in the other direction.

The bookkeeper is a role that did not exist when #1866 was written. **The bookkeeper does not get Budget Status.** `bookkeeperNav` is deliberately not `billingManagerNav`, and the role matrix asserts the bookkeeper is outside `CAN_VIEW_BUDGETS`. Do not add the leaf to their nav and do not widen that capability.

## Slice 4 note — the role change, restated

The original Slice 4 gives `irrigation_manager` full budget access and `field_tech` a server-side-restricted view returning `{ customerId, customerName, status, fillPercent }` and nothing else. Re-land it exactly as specced.

`CAN_VIEW_BUDGETS` (`lib/shared/src/roles.ts:189-195`) exists in main and currently mirrors the old allowlist. Either add `irrigation_manager` there and wire `budget-routes.ts` to `hasCapability`, or widen the local allowlist — pick one and say which. Do not leave the capability and the route disagreeing.

Financial Pulse access for the irrigation manager is handled by its own ticket (`FIX - Irrigation Manager Financial Pulse Access`) and is not part of this change.

That is correct behavior for an empty registry, and it is why nobody noticed the feature disappear. The page is only useful once annual goals are entered — see the bulk-entry ticket and the data-entry step below.

## Data entry is part of shipping this

Restoring the code does not give the manager anything. Roughly 50 customers need a 2026 annual goal entered before a single bar renders. Sequence:

1. This restore ships.
2. Goals are entered — by hand in the customer profile, or with the tool from `BUILD - Annual Budget Goal Bulk Paste Entry`.
3. Only then is the page verified against a customer in each of Go / Slow down / Stop / Unset.

The Rule 7 screenshot for this ticket must show **real allocations**, not the all-Unset state the recovered screenshots show. A restored page that renders Unset for everyone proves the code is back; it does not prove the feature works.

## Multi-tenancy (Rule 1)

Unchanged from the original spec and must be re-verified, not assumed. Every read is company-scoped from the caller's company, including the roll-up header, which must never aggregate across companies. `super_admin` passes an explicit company. The tech endpoint is scoped identically — a restricted payload is still a tenant-scoped one.

## Decisions

- **Recover from `608393a0`; do not re-implement** (decided).
- **Route and nav re-applied by hand against drifted files** (decided).
- **The bookkeeper does not get Budget Status** (decided) — a role that postdates the original spec.

## Done looks like

- A manager opens Manager Workspace and sees which customers are over budget without clicking anything.
- `/budget-status` is reachable from the sidebar for admin, billing manager and irrigation manager, and shows every customer ranked by budget risk against a company roll-up.
- A crew sees Go / Slow down / Stop per customer on a phone with no dollar figures anywhere.
- Work done but not yet billed is its own bar segment — the reason this was raised in the first place.
- The bookkeeper cannot reach the page and is not offered it in nav.
- Nothing on either surface edits a budget; every row links to the customer profile budget anchor.
