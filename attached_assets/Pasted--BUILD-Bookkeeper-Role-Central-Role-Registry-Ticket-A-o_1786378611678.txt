# BUILD — Bookkeeper Role + Central Role Registry

**Ticket A of 3.** Followed by `BUILD - AR Collections View on the Invoice List (B of 3)` and `BUILD - Invoice Payment Reminders + AR Notes (C of 3)`. Ship A first — B and C both depend on the capability sets it creates.

**What & Why**
There is no user type for a bookkeeper. Today the only way to give someone invoice visibility is to make them a `billing_manager`, which also hands them the power to create, edit, correct, void, and merge invoices and to approve estimates and parts. That is far more authority than the job needs.

This ticket creates the **`bookkeeper`** role and, first, the thing that makes adding a role safe: a single place where role membership is declared. It also closes a pre-existing hole where **any authenticated user can list every invoice in their company.**

When this ships, a bookkeeper can log in, see a working navigation, and read invoices. The AR-specific columns, filters, and reminders come in B and C.

## Current state (confirmed in code)

**Roles are not modelled anywhere.** `users.role` is a plain `text` column with `default("field_tech")` and a **comment** listing the five valid values (`schema.ts:37`). No enum, no constraint, no shared constant. The only type-level declaration is a union on a React prop (`company-admin-app.tsx:94`).

**Role checks are hand-rolled and scattered.** Non-test references to `billing_manager`: **104 server-side**, **162 client-side** across 25+ files. The pattern is a local array — `const allowedRoles = ['company_admin', 'super_admin', 'billing_manager', 'irrigation_manager']` (`parts-routes.ts:122`, and again at `:203`, `:261`, `:1105`) — or a direct string compare.

**`requireBillingAccess` (`routes.ts:518`) conflates reading and writing.** It hardcodes `company_admin || billing_manager` and guards invoice PDFs, `pdf/send` (`:12314`), monthly invoice creation (`:6563`), bill-separately (`:7197`), and the merge routes (`:7471`) — a mix of reads and mutations behind one gate.

**An unknown role gets a blank app.** `navigation.tsx:117` is a `switch (userRole)` with one case per role and `default: return []` (`:262`). A `bookkeeper` user with no case added sees **no navigation at all**. `App.tsx:357` separately branches on `user.role === "billing_manager"` to pick a shell.

**`GET /api/invoices` has no role gate** (`routes.ts:10110`) — `requireAuthentication` only. It is correctly company-scoped, but **any authenticated user, including `field_tech`, can list every invoice in their company with totals.** Pre-existing; this ticket closes it.

## Scope of the role (decided)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Can read** | Invoices, invoice PDFs, line items, payment status, balances, aging, customer billing contact and terms |
| **Can write** | Mark an invoice sent. Send a payment reminder (built in C). Add an AR note (built in C). Nothing else. |
| **Cannot** | Create, edit, correct, void, merge, or delete an invoice. Record or adjust a payment. Approve estimates or parts. Touch wet checks, work orders, billing sheets, or pricing. |
| **Cannot see** | Budgets, part costs, margins, labor rates, Financial Pulse, wet-check billing internals |

**Payments stay read-only from QuickBooks** (decided). The bookkeeper never marks an invoice paid in IrrigoPro. QBO is the payment system of record and `qb-payment-sync` is the only writer of `paymentStatus` / `balance` / `paidAt`. A manual override here would fight the sync on the next poll and produce exactly the kind of two-writers drift this project has been cleaning up.

## Slice 1 — The role registry

New module `lib/shared/src/roles.ts`, exported from `lib/shared/src/index.ts`:

- `ROLES` — the canonical list, `bookkeeper` included.
- `type Role` — the union, replacing the inline one at `company-admin-app.tsx:94`.
- **Named capability sets, not role lists at call sites**: `CAN_READ_INVOICES`, `CAN_EDIT_INVOICES`, `CAN_SEND_INVOICE_EMAIL`, `CAN_APPROVE_ESTIMATES`, `CAN_APPROVE_PARTS`, `CAN_VIEW_BUDGETS`, `CAN_VIEW_COSTS`.
- `hasCapability(role, capability): boolean`.

`bookkeeper` is in `CAN_READ_INVOICES` and `CAN_SEND_INVOICE_EMAIL`. It is in **none** of the others.

## Slice 2 — Split the gate, migrate the invoice call sites only

`requireBillingAccess` splits in two:

- `requireInvoiceRead` → `CAN_READ_INVOICES` (adds `bookkeeper`)
- `requireInvoiceWrite` → `CAN_EDIT_INVOICES` (unchanged membership: `company_admin`, `billing_manager`)

Every current `requireBillingAccess` site must be classified as one or the other **explicitly, one at a time**. **Default to write when ambiguous** — a wrongly-read-gated mutation is a privilege escalation; a wrongly-write-gated read is a support ticket. List the classification of each site in the proof.

**Close the `GET /api/invoices` hole**: add `requireInvoiceRead` (`routes.ts:10110`).

**Before landing that gate, report who currently reads invoices.** If `field_tech` or `irrigation_manager` hits this endpoint from any existing surface, that is a finding to raise, not something to silently allow or silently break. Grep the client for callers and say what you found.

**Migrate the invoice and AR call sites in this ticket only.** Do not attempt all 266 — that is a separate refactor and would make this unreviewable. Leave a `TODO(roles)` marker on the rest and record the count before and after.

## Slice 3 — Make the role usable in the app

- Add a `bookkeeper` case to the `navigation.tsx` switch (`:117`). **Do not let it fall to `default: return []` (`:262`).** Nav items: Invoices (landing), Customers, QuickBooks. Nothing else.
- Add `bookkeeper` to the `App.tsx:357` shell branch — it uses the same shell as `billing_manager`.
- Replace the inline role union at `company-admin-app.tsx:94` with the shared `Role` type.
- The bookkeeper's landing route is `/invoices`.

**Gate: `pnpm typecheck` passes.** Widening the `Role` union will surface every exhaustive switch that does not handle `bookkeeper` — that is the point. Each one is a real decision. **Do not cast or add a fallthrough to silence one**; handle it and say what you chose.

## Multi-tenancy (Rule 1)

No change to isolation. `GET /api/invoices` already scopes via `authenticatedUserCompanyId` with the `super_admin → null` convention (`routes.ts:10114–10120`); adding the role gate must not alter that. A bookkeeper in company A must get nothing for a company B invoice id.

## Decisions

- **`bookkeeper` is a new role, not a variant of `billing_manager`** (decided) — the job is reading and chasing, not authoring.
- **Read + AR actions only** (decided) — no invoice edit, void, merge, or correction.
- **Payments remain read-only from QuickBooks** (decided) — one writer for payment state.
- **Capability sets, not role arrays** (decided) — call sites ask what a user can do, not who they are.
- **Only invoice/AR call sites migrate now** (decided) — the other ~250 get `TODO(roles)`.
- **Invoices and AR only** (decided) — no budgets, costs, margins, or Financial Pulse.

## Done looks like

- A bookkeeper logs in, sees a populated navigation, lands on `/invoices`, and can read invoices and their PDFs.
- She cannot create, edit, void, merge, or correct an invoice, and cannot record a payment — the controls are absent, **and the server refuses if the endpoint is called directly.**
- She cannot see budgets, costs, margins, or Financial Pulse.
- `GET /api/invoices` is role-gated; a role without `CAN_READ_INVOICES` is refused.
- Role membership for every invoice route is readable in one file.

## Tests

- **Role matrix**, asserted on the **server response**, not the rendered UI: `bookkeeper` gets 200 on invoice read, invoice PDF, and mark-sent; **403** on create, edit, correct, void, merge, delete, payment adjust, estimate approve, parts approve, budget read, cost read.
- `GET /api/invoices` refuses a role without `CAN_READ_INVOICES` — including the pre-existing `field_tech` case.
- Every route that previously used `requireBillingAccess` behaves identically for `company_admin` and `billing_manager` after the split (regression guard — the split must not change existing access).
- `hasCapability` returns false for an unrecognised role string, and for a null or undefined role.
- Nav returns a non-empty item list for `bookkeeper` (direct guard against the `default: return []` trap).
- Company isolation: a bookkeeper in company A gets nothing for an invoice id from company B.

Follow the existing role-matrix pattern in `artifacts/api-server/src/routes/billing-workspace-role-access.test.ts`.
