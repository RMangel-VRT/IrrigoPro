# BUILD — One Irrigation System Per Customer

**What & Why**
Today a customer's irrigation system is described by four separate stores that nothing reconciles: `irrigation_controllers` (canonical), `property_controllers` (legacy, half-maintained), `customers.total_controllers` (an integer that is wrong by design for branch-level controllers), and a disconnected Google-Sheets prototype (`property_zones` / `zones` / `field_work_sessions`) that has no tenancy at all. On top of that, every downstream record identifies a controller by a **letter string** rather than a foreign key — which is how a duplicate letter silently destroyed wet-check zone data (see `FIX - Controller Letter Is Real Data`).

This ticket makes **`irrigation_controllers` + `irrigation_profile_zones` the single source of truth** for what equipment a customer has, keys operational records to the controller **row**, and deletes the stores that compete with it.

Background: `AUDIT - Per-Customer Irrigation Data Model`.

## Sequencing

Build **after** `FIX - Controller Letter Is Real Data`, which adds the `letter` column, auto-assignment from A, editability, the unique index, and moves the wet-check submit path off `property_controllers`. That fix is the live-bug stopgap; this is the structural consolidation. Where the two overlap, this spec supersedes:

- The letter fix's unique index on `wet_check_zone_records (wet_check_id, controller_letter, zone_number)` is **replaced** by an index on `controller_id` (Slice 1 below).

## The target model

| Fact | Home | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A customer's controllers | `irrigation_controllers` | scoped (companyId, customerId, branchName) |
| A controller's identity in the field | `letter` (A–Z) | display label only, editable, unique per scope |
| A controller's zones | `irrigation_profile_zones` | row count must equal `total_zones` |
| How many controllers | `COUNT(*)` of `irrigation_controllers` | derived, never stored |
| Which controller a zone record belongs to | `wet_check_zone_records.controller_id` | foreign key |
| Which controller a document referred to | frozen `controller_letter` text + nullable `controller_id` | historical snapshot |

## Slice 1 — Zone records key on the controller row

Add to `wet_check_zone_records` (`schema.ts:1452`):

- `controllerId: integer("controller_id").references(() => irrigationControllers.id)` — the real link.
- Replace `uniq_wet_check_zone` (currently `(wetCheckId, controllerLetter, zoneNumber)`, `schema.ts:1476`) with **`(wetCheckId, controllerId, zoneNumber)`**.
- Keep `controller_letter` as a denormalized display label so existing reads and PDFs keep working, but it stops being an identity.

After this, two controllers sharing a letter is merely cosmetic — their zone records are still distinct rows. The class of bug that caused this ticket becomes structurally impossible rather than index-prevented.

**Blast radius is small** — findings (`wet_check_findings.zoneRecordId`, `schema.ts:1489`) and zone photos (`:1627`) already reference zone records by foreign key, so nothing downstream needs to change. Only six places match zone records by letter and need rewriting:

- `storage.ts:7864` and `:8003` — mark zone records not-applicable when a controller is removed
- `storage.ts:8603` — ordering (order by controller `letter`, then `zoneNumber`, via a join)
- `storage.ts:8743` — the implicit-N/A fill (already being rewritten by the letter fix's Slice 4)
- `storage.ts:9281` — upsert-match on insert
- `scripts/backfill-trim-phantom-zones.ts:176`

**Backfill:** for every existing zone record, resolve `controller_id` by matching `(wet_check.companyId, wet_check.customerId, wet_check.branchName, controller_letter)` against `irrigation_controllers.letter`. Records that resolve to **more than one** controller (the collided ones) or to **none** must be reported, not guessed — write them to the migration output with wet check id, letter, and zone number. Do not create the new unique index until the backfill leaves zero ambiguous rows.

## Slice 2 — Documents keep their snapshot, gain a link

`controller_letter` is stored as text on six document tables: `billing_sheets` (`schema.ts:241`), `estimates` (`:503`), `estimate_items` (`:578`), `work_orders` (`:770`), `work_order_items` (`:843`), `work_order_zone_photos` (`:1652`).

- **Leave the existing text alone.** An issued estimate must keep saying what it said when it was issued; re-lettering a controller two years later must not silently change a historical document.
- Add a **nullable** `controller_id` to each, populated going forward at creation time from the controller the record was derived from.
- No backfill of past documents, and no rewriting of past letters. The nullable column simply starts filling in.
- Rendering keeps using the stored text. `controller_id` is for traceability and future reporting, not display.

## Slice 3 — Retire `property_controllers`

The letter fix's Slice 4 already moves the wet-check submit path onto `irrigation_controllers`. That leaves `setCustomerControllerCount` (`storage.ts:7955`) and `ensurePropertyControllers` (`:7752`) as the last writers, plus the legacy fallback in `buildWetCheckGrid` (`wet-check-grid.ts:69`).

- Migrate `setCustomerControllerCount` to operate on `irrigation_controllers`: shrinking removes controllers by letter within scope, keeps the existing `ControllerHasZonesError` confirmation guard, and keeps the behaviour of marking zone records on in-progress wet checks as `not_applicable` (`storage.ts:7991`) — now resolved by `controller_id`.
- Delete `ensurePropertyControllers` and the legacy branch of `buildWetCheckGrid`. Note the two defaults that never agreed — column default **100** (`schema.ts:1363`) vs inserted **12** (`storage.ts:7774`); both disappear with the table.
- Drop the `property_controllers` table **last**, after the letter backfill has consumed its `controller_letter` values (the letter fix uses them as backfill input).

## Slice 4 — `customers.total_controllers` becomes derived

`schema.ts:81`, synced only when `branchName === ""` (`irrigation-profile-routes.ts:351`, `:481`; `storage.ts:8018`), so it is wrong by construction for any customer with branch-level controllers — yet it drives the controller count in `irrigation-system-card.tsx:42` and the fallback count in `wet-check-grid.ts:71`.

- Every read becomes `COUNT(*)` of `irrigation_controllers` for the relevant (company, customer, branch) scope.
- Remove the three sync writes.
- Leave the column in place for one release (stop writing, stop reading), then drop it in a follow-up so a rollback doesn't lose anything.

## Slice 5 — Delete the `property_zones` prototype (decided: delete outright)

Three tables with **no `company_id` column at all** — `property_zones` (`schema.ts:586`), `zones` (`:595`), `field_work_sessions` (`:604`) — exposed by `GET /api/property-zones` (`routes.ts:8228`) under `requireAuthentication` with no tenant filter. Any authenticated user of any company can read every company's property list and addresses. The headline feature never worked: `syncPropertyZonesFromGoogleSheets` (`storage.ts:3098`) logs the URL and returns.

Delete:

- Tables `property_zones`, `zones`, `field_work_sessions` and their insert/select types.
- Routes `/api/property-zones` (GET list, GET by id, POST, POST sync-google-sheets) at `routes.ts:8228`–`:8280`, plus the `/api/field-work-sessions` routes those pages post to.
- Storage methods `getPropertyZones`, `getPropertyZone`, `createPropertyZone`, `updatePropertyZone`, `deletePropertyZone`, `syncPropertyZonesFromGoogleSheets`, `getZones`, `createZone`, `updateZone`, `deleteZone`, and the field-work-session methods (`storage.ts:3066`–`~3130`).
- Pages `pages/field-tech.tsx` (416 lines) and `pages/field-portal.tsx` (326 lines), and their routes in `App.tsx:510`, `:525` and `company-admin-app.tsx:195`, `:229`.
- The `propertyZones` / `zones` seed data in `seed.ts:122`, `:142`.

Neither page appears in the sidebar (`components/layout`), so nothing in navigation breaks — they were orphans under Rule 3. Verify no other component imports them before removing, and confirm nothing else references `field_work_sessions`.

## Do not touch: site maps

The `controllers` and `irrigation_zones` tables back the live `/site-maps` feature. They are **explicitly out of scope for this ticket and are not to be modified, linked, renamed, or deleted.**

- Do not add a foreign key from them to `irrigation_controllers`.
- Do not change their columns, routes (`site-map-routes.ts`), storage methods, or the `/site-maps` and `/customers/:id/site-maps` pages.
- Do not treat them as duplicates to clean up. They are a separate, working feature.

They model the same physical equipment as the irrigation profile and can disagree with it. That is a known, accepted condition and is not being addressed now.

## Multi-tenancy (Rule 1)

Every new lookup resolves scope from the record's own (company, customer, branch) — never from caller context. The zone-record backfill joins through `wet_checks` to get company and customer, so a letter can never resolve to another company's controller. Slice 5 removes the only irrigation-area endpoints that had no tenant filter at all.

## Decisions

- **Zone records key on `controller_id`; letter is a label** (decided) — makes the collision structurally impossible.
- **Documents freeze their letter text, gain a nullable `controller_id`** (decided) — history stays honest.
- **`property_controllers` dropped** (decided) — after the letter backfill consumes its values.
- **`total_controllers` derived** (decided) — stop writing, stop reading, drop the column later.
- **`property_zones` prototype deleted outright** (decided, no row count) — closes the tenancy hole.
- **Site maps are not part of this work** (decided) — `controllers` / `irrigation_zones` and the `/site-maps` feature stay exactly as they are.

## Done looks like

- `irrigation_controllers` + `irrigation_profile_zones` are the only description of a customer's system.
- A wet-check zone record points at a controller row; duplicate letters cannot merge zone data.
- Historical estimates, work orders, and billing sheets still display the letter they were issued with.
- No table named `property_controllers`, `property_zones`, `zones`, or `field_work_sessions` remains.
- Controller counts are computed, never stored, and are correct for branch-level customers.
- No endpoint in the irrigation area returns data without a company filter.

## Tests

- Two controllers deliberately given the same letter → their zone records remain distinct rows; both are addressable; findings attach to the right one.
- Re-lettering a controller → live zone records follow it (they key on id); an already-issued estimate still shows the old letter.
- Deleting a controller with zones → confirmation guard still fires; on confirm, in-progress wet-check zone records for that controller become `not_applicable`, resolved by `controller_id`.
- Zone-record backfill: unambiguous rows resolve; ambiguous and unresolvable rows are **reported and the unique index is not created** until zero remain.
- Controller count for a customer with both customer-level and branch-level controllers is correct on every surface.
- Company isolation: the backfill never resolves a letter to another company's controller; a customer with the same letters in two companies stays separate.
- After Slice 5, `/api/property-zones` returns 404 and no import of the deleted pages remains.
